# Independent RQ1 audit

**Verdict:** The frozen scores and the direction of the frozen comparison are defensible for this shipment dataset and this synthetic typo protocol. The 30% result passes the prespecified one-sided, Holm-adjusted test against cleaned-text exact matching. The full H1, which covers all three positive noise levels and a 0.90 F1 target at 5%, was not met. Confidence is high in the arithmetic and replayed implementation, and limited for inference beyond this dataset. The strongest concerns are incomplete historical provenance, split sensitivity, and an outdated reproduction command. [Design](RQ1_CONTEXT.md#2-study-design-fixed-do-not-change-without-asking) · [Owner decisions](ARCHITECTURE.md#owner-decisions) · [Independent paired statistics](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/paired_statistics.csv)

## Safe to claim in viva

- **On this dataset and typo protocol**, primary single-cutoff fuzzy F1 was 0.7856, 0.7697 and 0.7565 at 5/15/30%. Cleaned exact was 0.9094, 0.8113 and 0.6491. Only the 30% directional comparison passed the planned adjustment. [Frozen outcome](RQ1_RESULTS.md#observed-outcome-for-h1) · [Paired statistics](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/paired_statistics.csv)
- Fuzzy had higher recall and less fragmentation at all three positive levels, while cleaned exact maintained precision 1.000 versus fuzzy precision near .70. [Independent aggregate checks](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/claims.log)
- Composite had higher mean fuzzy F1 by .0333–.0455 across four levels. The post-hoc field tests did not clear .05. [Ablation](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/field_set_ablation.csv)
- A separate seed-0 scratch replay matched the frozen results and sampled independent scores matched to floating-point precision. [Replay](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/replay.log) · [Metrics](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/metrics.log)

## Do NOT claim

- The full H1 holds, fuzzy dominates exact across typo levels, or fuzzy achieved .90 at 5% in the frozen experiment. The frozen 5% and 15% point estimates favor cleaned exact. [Frozen outcome](RQ1_RESULTS.md#observed-outcome-for-h1)
- Near-perfect post-hoc blocking is a validated operational result, or its gain isolates the effect of Bloom encoding. Blocking has clean proxy-truth fields and P2 changes evaluation composition. [Post-hoc limitations](RQ1_RESULTS.md#post-hoc-analyses-designed-after-seeing-the-frozen-results)
- Fragmentation 1.0 establishes correct linking. The counterexample has precision .2667. [Counterexample](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/claims.log)
- Ten independent real-world replications, inputs historically pinned by SHA-256, or privacy demonstrated by RQ1. Seeds repeatedly sample one dataset, hashes are absent from frozen metadata, and differential privacy is outside the design. [Split and metadata checks](../backend/app/results/rq1_audit/artifacts.log) · [Design](RQ1_CONTEXT.md#1-project)

## Overall confidence and limits

The frozen result is defensible as a **within-dataset, repeated-split experiment**. The score arithmetic, sampled prediction-to-score calculations, saved cutoffs and seed-0 replay agree. The main design has no detected within-seed answer-key leakage. The primary conclusion should specify the one-sided test, cleaned-text comparator and synthetic noise.
