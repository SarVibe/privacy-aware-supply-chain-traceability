# RQ2 v2 empirical collision benchmark

## V1 recap and audit findings

V1 found that shorter filters raised raw bit-membership hit rate and ambiguity. Its full-universe random baseline used a wider token pool than the aligner, and the saturated cell produced one cluster in every seed. The V1 post-hoc supplement reports excess and lift from saved CSVs. The ephemeral V1 key prevents recovery of matched nulls and known-key metrics.

## V2 rationale

v2 endpoints were defined after observing v1 results and are a methodological refinement, not independent confirmation of the original hypotheses.

## Protocol

Frozen protocol commit `b5d7238e77f691ecb8755ff7f517400625db3fc1`. The main grid used seeds [5, 6, 7, 8, 9], 15% description noise, entity-disjoint validation/test/reference within each seed, and a fixed environment-derived research key. Each cell selected tau on validation. Test metrics and the above-ceiling sensitivity were reported separately. All measurements are empirical.

## Limitations

- The evaluation measures empirical privacy under two specific endpoints on one dataset. RQ3 addresses formal privacy.
- The seeds are overlapping resamples. The overlap table records pairwise test overlap and pilot-to-test overlap.
- Occupancy and ambiguity depend on composite Q. Under description-only noise, clean dosage, form and pack fields also contribute to the ground truth. The all-fields row uses separately generated observations.
- The known-key calculation uses the evaluator's favorable target-plus-reference vocabulary. A candidate set describes token membership under that vocabulary and does not establish recovery of source records or identities.
- The pilot checked correctness and runtime. V2 endpoints and hypotheses were chosen after V1 results.
